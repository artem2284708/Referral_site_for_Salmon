Who Can Join?

To ensure the best experience for all participants, we have the following requirements:

Small Business Owner

Own or manage a small business in the Philippines. This includes Moto/cars, Appliances, Furniture, Clothes, DYI. Also, your turnover is over 100.000 - 1.000.000 ₱/month

6+ Months in Business

Your business should be operational for at least 6 months. Your business is registered and you have such documentation as BIR, DTI, ID, Business Permit, bank account.

Offline Sales

Have physical sales transactions or in-person customer interactions. This includes physical stores, markets.
